Student Builder. collector of unfinished prototypes.

I like projects that force me to learn five different skills at once.

One week I'm reverse engineering a camera. The next I'm designing a PCB, building a combat robot, experimenting with drones, writing software, or turning old hardware into something it was never designed to be.

My interests sit at the intersection of:

* Aerospace engineering
* Robotics
* Embedded systems
* Hardware product design
* Computer vision
* Technical research
* Storytelling and filmmaking

I care less about following a predefined path and more about building difficult things that teach me something new.

Current mission: become the kind of engineer who can design the electronics, write the software, build the mechanism, document the process, and explain why it works.

If a project requires soldering, CAD, code, machining, research papers, and several bad decisions, I'm probably interested.
